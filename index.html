Facts

What is actually there

Plain answers. Earnings vary and are never guaranteed.

Is the income guaranteed?

No. What you earn depends on how much you wait and on available revenue. The figures on this page are illustrative examples.

Which AI apps are supported?

Claude, Gemini, Grok, and ChatGPT.

What can the extension see?

It only needs to detect when a response is loading. This page does not yet publish a full privacy policy; read it before installing once it is available.

When and how do payouts work?

Payout terms will be published at launch. Until then, balances on this page are demo values.

Is the extension available now?

The Chrome Web Store listing is not live yet. The Add to Chrome button will open it once it is.
